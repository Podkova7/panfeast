**If you cannot download Meta Muse, check regional availability and device compatibility before troubleshooting your phone.** Being able to open a US app listing does not establish that the app is offered to your account.

Meta's [September 8 launch announcement](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/introducing-muse-personal-ai-agent/) describes a US rollout on iOS, Android and the web. We have not independently verified access in every country as of **September 30, 2026**. No worldwide launch date is established by that announcement.

## App installation and account access are separate

There are three useful questions to answer in order:

1. **Can your device install the app?** Check the operating-system requirement in the actual store listing.
2. **Does your storefront offer it?** Read the message shown by your local store, rather than relying on a shared US link.
3. **Does your account have access?** Follow the app or website's access instructions after signing in.

Passing the first check does not guarantee the third. If the app opens but presents an access notice, reinstalling it may leave you at exactly the same notice.

## What about the UK, Europe and other countries?

The launch announcement confirms the US rollout; it does not provide a complete current country list. We therefore cannot label the UK, Bulgaria, India or any other unverified market as supported or assign it a release date.

Check two places: Meta's current product page and your local app store. If they give different impressions, distinguish a general marketing page from the specific access decision shown to your account.

The languages an app lists and the regions where accounts can use it are different information. A language being supported does not establish availability in the country where that language is spoken.

## Diagnose the message you actually see

| What you see | What to check next |
| --- | --- |
| An incompatible-device message | Compare your installed OS with the listing's requirement |
| An unavailable-country message | Check Meta's rollout information and your storefront region |
| An access notice or waitlist | Follow the instructions attached to that notice |
| A normal sign-in error | Check the account you selected and its sign-in recovery options |
| An installed app that will not open | Note the app version, OS version and exact error before troubleshooting |

This table is a diagnostic approach, not a statement that every account encounters these screens. We have not tested each onboarding route.

Do not change several things at once. If you update the operating system, switch accounts and reinstall simultaneously, you will have less information about which change affected the problem.

## Do you need an invitation code?

We have not verified a universal invitation requirement or a reliable public code-distribution route. Follow the official access flow shown to your account.

A stranger offering a code cannot establish that your region or account will become eligible. Do not pay for an access promise based solely on a social post. If the service presents a legitimate invitation field, use the accompanying official instructions.

## Does WhatsApp access mean everyone can use Muse?

Meta describes messaging Muse through WhatsApp as an access route. That does not make an existing Meta AI conversation proof of Muse eligibility. Check the product name and the access instructions rather than assuming every AI chat belongs to the new agent.

## Once you have access

Begin with one task whose output you can inspect without connecting personal accounts. Our [Muse prompt guide](/meta-muse-prompts-first-tasks/) includes shopping comparisons, travel research and planning examples, plus what to check in each result.

Save permission-heavy tasks for when you understand the product and have a specific reason to connect a service.
