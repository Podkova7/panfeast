When resting on a nightstand or workspace charging dock, an idle smartphone traditionally turns into an uninformative black slab of glass. Recognizing this unused surface real estate, Apple introduced **StandBy mode** in iOS. StandBy transforms your charging iPhone into an ambient, glanceable smart display that automatically activates whenever the device is positioned in landscape orientation while connected to power.

Whether you want a full-screen retro clock, a dual-widget productivity dashboard, a rotating family photo album, or a live scoreboard, StandBy delivers tailored information without requiring you to touch or unlock the phone. When paired with an Always-On display on supported Pro models, StandBy remains permanently visible, while standard models wake intelligently with gentle motion detection. This tutorial explains how to enable, configure, and customize StandBy mode for your bedroom, kitchen, and workspace.

## Understanding StandBy Display Architectures

StandBy is organized into three primary horizontal views that you can cycle through by swiping left or right across the screen:

1. **Widget View (Default Dual-Column):** Two independent Smart Stacks side by side. You can place an interactive Reminders checklist on the left and a detailed weather forecast on the right.
2. **Photo View:** A full-screen digital picture frame that curates moments from your iCloud Photos library, including featured pets, nature landscapes, and city trips.
3. **Clock View:** Five distinct full-screen clock faces (Analog, Digital, World, Solar, and Float) that turn your iPhone into a design-forward alarm clock.

## Step-by-Step: Enabling and Customizing StandBy Mode

Configuring StandBy takes only moments inside iOS Settings:

### Step 1: Verification of System Settings
1. Open the **Settings** app on your iPhone.
2. Scroll down and tap **StandBy**.
3. Toggle **StandBy** to the **On** position.
4. If you use an iPhone with an Always-On display, review the **Display** submenu:
   - **Always On:** Keeps the display subtly active indefinitely.
   - **Night Mode:** Shifts the entire interface to an eye-soothing monochromatic red tint in dark rooms to preserve dark adaptation and melatonin production.
   - **Motion to Wake:** Automatically awakens the display when the TrueDepth sensor detects room movement or vibrations at night.

### Step 2: Triggering StandBy Mode Physically
To activate StandBy, your iPhone must meet three physical conditions simultaneously:
1. Connected to power (via MagSafe, Qi wireless charging pad, or standard USB-C / Lightning cable).
2. Placed stationary in **landscape (horizontal)** orientation.
3. Tilted at an upright angle (resting flat on a table will not activate StandBy; a magnetic MagSafe stand or angled kickstand is ideal).

Once in place, press the side button or let the display settle; within two seconds, the StandBy interface illuminates automatically.

## Customizing the Dual-Widget Smart Stacks

The dual-widget view is the most versatile mode for office desks and workstation docks:

1. While in StandBy, swipe horizontally until you reach the dual-column widget screen.
2. Long-press either the left or right widget column to enter edit mode (you will be prompted to authenticate via Face ID).
3. Swipe vertically within the stack to browse available widgets, or tap the **+ (Plus)** button in the top-left corner to add new widgets from installed apps.
4. Select high-utility widgets such as **Calendar Up Next**, **Battery Levels**, **Stocks Watchlist**, or **HomeKit Scenes**.
5. Toggle **Smart Rotate** and **Widget Suggestions** on or off based on whether you prefer iOS to dynamically predict what you need or keep a rigid manual order.
6. Tap **Done** in the top-right corner to lock in your layout.

## Configuring Night Mode and Ambient Sleep Protection

Bright blue and white light disrupts circadian rhythms when checking the time at 3:00 AM. Apple engineered StandBy with dedicated optical sleep protections:

- **Red Tint Monochrome:** When the ambient light sensor detects that room lights have been turned off, StandBy seamlessly transitions all on-screen graphics into pure red tones. Red wavelengths do not constrict pupils or interfere with night vision.
- **Vibration-Based Waking:** On iPhone models without an Always-On display, you do not need to tap the screen directly. Simply tapping your nightstand surface creates enough micro-vibration for the accelerometer to wake the display for six seconds.
- **Notification Privacy:** In **Settings > StandBy > Show Notifications**, you can choose to hide sensitive notification previews until your face is recognized by Face ID, ensuring roommates or family members cannot read incoming texts.

For official feature specifications and MagSafe accessory standards, visit [Apple Support](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/use-standby-iph878f77232/ios).
