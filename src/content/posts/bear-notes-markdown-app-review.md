Writing on digital devices often feels encumbered by bloated toolbars, clunky ribbon menus, and slow web technologies that distract from pure linguistic composition. For years, **Bear** has stood as the gold standard for minimalist Markdown writing on Apple hardware. With the release of **Bear 2**, the independent development team at Shiny Frog rebuilt the app's foundational typography engine, delivering the most refined writing sanctuary available on macOS, iOS, and iPadOS.

Bear 2 introduces native Markdown tables, collapsible header folding, inline nested footnotes, end-to-end encrypted notes, and complete Apple Pencil sketch integration—all while preserving the fast, distraction-free aesthetic that made the original app beloved. In this comprehensive review, we evaluate Bear 2's custom typography engine, nested hashtag hierarchy, iCloud sync architecture, and subscription pricing.

## The Architecture: Custom Markdown Rendering Engine

The centerpiece of Bear 2 is its ground-up typography engine:

1. **Inline Markdown Hiding:** Traditional markdown editors force users to look at unsightly formatting symbols (`**bold**`, `# headers`, `*italics*`). Bear 2 implements seamless inline styling: formatting syntax stays hidden until your text cursor rests directly on the word, providing a clean typographic preview without losing plain-text portable integrity.
2. **Collapsible Header Folding:** Long-form essays, book chapters, and research treatises can be folded under any H1, H2, or H3 heading with a single tap, allowing writers to focus on one section at a time without endless vertical scrolling.
3. **Private iCloud CoreData Sync:** Bear does not maintain proprietary user databases or log credentials on private cloud servers. All synchronization takes place directly between your personal devices using Apple's private, end-to-end encrypted iCloud CloudKit infrastructure.

## Key Features That Make Bear 2 Exceptional

### 1. Flexible Nested Hashtag Taxonomy
Bear rejects rigid, nested directory folders in favor of a dynamic hashtag taxonomy:
- Inserting a hashtag anywhere inside a document (e.g., `#finance/taxes/2025`) instantly generates an organized sidebar folder hierarchy.
- A single note can live in multiple categories simultaneously simply by adding additional tags, eliminating the dilemma of where to file multifaceted research.
- Bear allows you to assign custom icons and colors to specific tag categories for rapid visual identification.

To explore building a complete personal knowledge architecture, read our [Apple Notes PKM System Guide](/apple-notes-pkm-organization-system/).

### 2. Comprehensive Table Support and Footnotes
One of the most requested features in Bear's history was table support:
- Bear 2 introduces an intuitive, responsive table engine. You can drag and reorder rows and columns effortlessly, format individual cells with bold or monospace code fonts, and export tables directly to CSV.
- Academic researchers and essayists can insert footnotes that fold neatly into superscript references and aggregate automatically at the document footer.

### 3. Native OCR Search and PDF Indexing
Bear 2 indexes text embedded inside images, screenshots, and attached PDF documents using the on-device Apple Neural Engine:
- Searching for a keyword in your Bear search bar instantly surfaces receipts, book photos, and scanned whiteboard diagrams containing that text.
- All OCR processing takes place locally on your iPhone or Mac without uploading images to cloud servers.

## Pricing Model: A Model of Developer Respect

In an era of predatory software pricing where utility apps demand $15 to $30 per month, Shiny Frog maintains an extraordinarily fair, accessible subscription model:
- **Free Core App:** Complete access to the Markdown editor, tables, backlinks, and export tools on a single device with zero ads.
- **Bear Pro ($2.99 / month or $29.99 / year):** Unlocks instant iCloud synchronization across iPhone, iPad, and Mac, encrypted note locking with Face ID / Touch ID, custom themes, and premium PDF / Word export presets.

A single Bear Pro subscription covers all your Apple devices under Apple Family Sharing, making it one of the absolute best values in the entire App Store ecosystem.

## The Final Verdict

Bear 2 is a triumph of thoughtful software craftsmanship. By respecting Apple design aesthetics, prioritizing user privacy, and engineering a world-class typography engine, Shiny Frog created the ultimate digital sanctuary for writers, journalists, and thinkers.
