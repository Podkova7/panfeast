Extracting text from a printed document, copying a Wi-Fi password printed on the back of a router, or translating a restaurant menu in a foreign country historically required downloading third-party scanning apps filled with intrusive ads and subscription prompts. With **Live Text** and **Visual Look Up**, Apple built optical character recognition (OCR) and computer vision machine learning models directly into iOS.

Operating 100% on-device via the Apple Neural Engine, Live Text recognizes typography across photos, screenshots, paused video frames, and the live Camera viewfinder. Concurrently, Visual Look Up identifies plants, animal breeds, famous architectural landmarks, artwork, food recipes, and laundry care tags. In this guide, we break down how to utilize these computer vision tools to accelerate daily research and capture information effortlessly.

## The Architecture of On-Device Computer Vision in iOS

Unlike cloud-based image recognition services that upload your private camera stream to remote servers, Apple's implementation prioritizes user privacy:

1. **Neural Engine CoreML Acceleration:** All pixel segmentation, character recognition, and entity classification are processed locally using deep learning convolutional neural networks.
2. **Universal System Integration:** Live Text is not confined to the Camera app. It operates natively in Photos, Safari, Quick Look, Files, Screenshot previews, and paused Safari videos.
3. **Contextual Action Parsers:** When Live Text isolates text, it parses the semantic meaning. Phone numbers present a "Call" button; street addresses present "Open in Maps"; currencies offer instant conversion; and foreign words offer one-tap inline translation.

## Step-by-Step: Mastering Live Text Across iOS

Here is how to extract and utilize text from any physical or digital source:

### Method 1: Using Live Text in the Real-Time Camera
1. Launch the native **Camera** app on your iPhone.
2. Point your camera at any printed document, business card, whiteboard, or package shipping label.
3. As soon as text is detected, a yellow bounding box brackets the typography, and a **Live Text icon** (three horizontal lines enclosed in a square) illuminates in the bottom-right corner.
4. Tap the **Live Text icon**: iOS freezes the typography on screen.
5. Tap or drag your finger across the words to select them just like normal text on a webpage.
6. Select **Copy**, **Look Up**, **Translate**, or tap an active phone number or URL to launch it immediately.

### Method 2: Extracting Text from Paused Video Frames
1. Play any video in the Photos app or in native Safari.
2. Pause the video at the exact moment a sign, book page, or computer terminal code appears.
3. Tap the **Live Text icon** in the bottom-right corner of the video player.
4. Highlight the displayed code or text strings and tap **Copy** to paste directly into your notes or terminal.

## Step-by-Step: Utilizing Visual Look Up for Objects and Symbols

Visual Look Up turns your camera roll into an encyclopedic encyclopedia:

### 1. Identifying Flora, Fauna, and Landmarks
1. Take or open a photo of a flower, dog, cat, bird, statue, or historic monument in the **Photos** app.
2. Examine the **Info button (i)** at the bottom toolbar:
   - If Visual Look Up identifies a recognizable entity, the Info icon displays glowing sparkle stars (✨).
3. Tap the sparkling **Info button** or swipe up on the image.
4. A card appears: tap **Look Up - Plant**, **Look Up - Dog Breed**, or **Look Up - Landmark**.
5. iOS presents botanical taxonomy, breed characteristics, and Wikipedia summaries without leaving the app.

### 2. Decoding Laundry Care and Automotive Dashboard Icons
Visual Look Up is uniquely tuned to decode cryptic physical symbols:
1. Snap a close-up photo of the fabric care label on a piece of clothing.
2. Open the photo, tap the sparkling **Info icon**, and select **Look Up - Laundry Care**.
3. iOS displays the exact meaning of every symbol: washing temperature limits, bleaching prohibitions, tumble dry settings, and iron heat guidelines.
4. The same capability applies to instrument cluster warning lights on car dashboards.

To ensure your web browsing research remains private after identifying objects, consult [Safari iOS Privacy and Security Features](/safari-ios-privacy-security-features/).

## One-Tap Subject Isolation (Subject Lift)

Complementing Visual Look Up is the **Subject Lift** feature:
1. In Photos, Safari, or Quick Look, press and hold your finger on the main subject (person, pet, or product).
2. A glowing ripple of light contours the edge of the subject as the neural network separates it from the background.
3. Drag the cutout directly into Messages, Mail, or Notes, or release your finger and tap **Copy** or **Share**.
4. The resulting graphic is saved as a clean, transparent PNG sticker ready for presentations or messaging.
